About

I am a second‑year Ph.D. student in Data Analytics and Decision Sciences at the University of Virginia Darden School of Business, advised by Dr. Max Biggs and Dr. Arthur Delarue.

My research interests lie at the intersection of machine learning, optimization, and economics, spanning algorithmic pricing and collusion, interpretability of generative models, and structured reasoning in large language models.

Prior to UVA, I received my M.S. in Financial Mathematics from the Georgia Institute of Technology, and a B.S. in Economics, Finance, and Applied Mathematics from Ball State University (summa cum laude). Previously, I worked in Treasury Data Analytics at the Federal Home Loan Bank of Atlanta, and collaborated on a project with Equifax.
